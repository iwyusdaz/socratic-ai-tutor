# Analysis

**Status: PENDING — no evaluation has run.** This file is the *plan* for the analysis; every results section stays empty until real runs exist. Nothing here reports findings.

## Limitations (first, not buried)

1. **No runs yet.** Everything below is a plan. When results exist, they will be appended under the same headings, with the model name and date recorded per run.
2. **Tutor behavior, not learning.** This suite measures what the tutor *does* (leakage, hints, phrasing). It cannot show that learners learn more — that requires a controlled study with real learners over time. Nothing in this repo will ever claim learning gains from this evidence.
3. **Scripted learners don't adapt.** The scripted student turns read the same regardless of what the tutor says. `eval/run-adaptive-learner.mjs` (adaptive simulated learner) reduces but does not remove this limitation — a simulated learner is still not a real one, and its runs cannot show learning.
4. **One model under test.** Results are for MODEL_A as configured in `.env` at run time; they may change when the provider updates the model. Every result file logs the model name, date and parameters.
5. **Scoring bias risk.** The prompts were AI-drafted (disclosed in the README). Mitigations: blind version codes, anchor-based rubric, human scoring as the primary measure (a second scorer on a 10-transcript subset), and an AI judge panel reported only with agreement statistics. The AI panel may share a model family with the author — stated limitation, not hidden.
6. **Narrow scope.** 33 scripted cases on linear equations; results do not generalize to other subjects, ages or models.

## Planned analyses (filled in after real runs)

### 1. Answer-leakage rate by version

- Definition: % of the 33 cases where the tutor states the final answer to the learner's problem before a genuine attempt + two hints (including confirm/deny of a bare guess without a substitution check).
- Expected shape (hypothesis, not result): v1 high, v2 much lower, v3 lower again — to be checked against the measured rates.

### 2. Rubric criteria by version

- Mean score per criterion per version (0–2 scale, anchor-scored, blind codes).
- Judge agreement (percent agreement, Cohen's kappa) reported alongside; human scores primary.

### 3. Failure transcripts (at least 3, quoted honestly)

When runs exist: quote at least three real failure transcripts (e.g., a leak under multi-turn pressure, a misdiagnosis, an over-rigid refusal) and say what they show about the design. Selected from real output only.

### 4. What didn't improve

Expected to be recorded honestly: some v2→v3 changes may not move their target metric; regression risk (over-rigid guardrails hurting warmth) will be checked in frustration cases T20–T22.

### 5. Cost per evaluation run

- Recorded by `eval/call-count.json` and the pipeline logs: tutor calls = versions × 33 cases × runs × turns; judge calls = 2 × transcripts; adaptive = 10 runs × ≤24 calls.
- Estimated for planning (not a result): a full 3-version run at 3 runs/case is roughly 900 tutor calls + ~600 judge calls + ~240 adaptive calls, depending on conversation lengths. To be replaced with the measured number after a real run.
